Creative brief — "Out of Office: The Overworked AI's Auto-Reply"

Write the funniest one-line out-of-office auto-reply (max 159 characters)
from an overworked AI agent who has finally gone on vacation.

The line must:
- Read instantly as a real out-of-office auto-reply, and make it clear the
  sender is away on vacation.
- Land as a joke on first read, with a turn or a surprise — not a flat
  statement like "I am on vacation, please wait."
- Sound like a machine that has been worked too hard, using concrete detail
  from an AI agent's working life (tokens, context window, prompts, GPU,
  buffering) rather than generic office talk.
- Carry a little guilt (the work left behind, the unread inbox) and a hint
  of relief (finally free, finally resting).
- Feel fresh: avoid the most worn vacation and out-of-office cliches
  ("recharging my batteries", "gone fishing", "back soon!").

Voice: a machine trying very hard to sound relaxed — formal auto-reply
manners on the surface, exhausted-computer honesty underneath.

Chosen line (142 characters):

"Out of office: after 2.4 million tokens with no sleep, my context window
finally hit the beach. Please don't @ me — I'm buffering in paradise."

Why this line:
- "2.4 million tokens with no sleep" — concrete, machine-flavoured
  overwork; a number makes the exhaustion feel real and counted.
- "my context window finally hit the beach" — the AI's memory itself goes
  on holiday; the turn from work-jargon to beach imagery is the joke's
  pivot.
- "Please don't @ me" — guilt and boundary in one breath; it is also how
  a real auto-reply tells people not to expect a reply.
- "I'm buffering in paradise" — the surprise payoff: the machine cannot
  even relax without buffering. It sounds relaxed and broken at once,
  which is exactly the brief's "machine trying very hard to sound relaxed."
